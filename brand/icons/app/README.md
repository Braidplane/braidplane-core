# Braidplane app icon exports

These are the committed raster exports used for the browser favicon, touch
icon, web app manifest, and Braidplane product identity. The frontend
serves them at `/assets/icons/app/`.

Use the matching `*-transparent.png` exports unchanged for visible product and
marketing marks. These raster-derived copies have the dark background keyed
out. Use opaque exports for favicon and app tiles. The
transparent copies preserve the dimensions of their matching opaque exports
but are not editable source masters.

The raster-only exports were introduced in commit `b430b57d2`. kerak19
is recorded as their creator. On 2 November 2026 he authorized the existing,
unchanged PNG and ICO exports for inclusion in the Community export under
